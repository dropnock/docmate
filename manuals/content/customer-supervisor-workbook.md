# DocMate Customer Portal — Supervisor Training Workbook

**For:** Customer supervisors in training · **Use with:** *Customer Portal — Supervisor User Manual*

This workbook turns the Supervisor User Manual into hands-on practice. Each module has:
- **what you'll learn** — the module's goals;
- **exercises** — practice in the training portal;
- **check your understanding** — questions to answer in the boxes provided;
- a **training record** at the end, where you and your trainer sign off each module.

Work through the modules in order. Each one builds on the one before. Allow about **4 hours** in total, or two half-day sessions.

| Trainee name | Trainer name | Start date | Training portal address |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

> **Training portal only.** Do every exercise in the training portal your trainer gives you, never in your live portal. Samples, assignments and remediation **can't be undone**, so practising on real lots would affect real work.

## Before you start (trainer setup)

*This page is for the trainer. Prepare it before the session.*

Each trainee needs these in the training portal:

| Item | Needed for | Ready |
|---|---|---|
| A **supervisor** account with a **temporary password** that hasn't been used | Module 1 | ☐ |
| At least one training **reviewer** account, preferably a reviewer trainee working through the *Reviewer Training Workbook* at the same time | Modules 6–8 | ☐ |
| A project on **ISO 2859‑1** sampling, with **three Released lots**, each of about 40 records | Modules 2–8, 11 | ☐ |
| At least one lot with **several outgoing inspection attempts** (failed, then passed) | Module 3 | ☐ |
| A record flagged by an indexer and **shared** with the customer; a second flag already **resolved** | Module 9 | ☐ |
| A record that customer QC has **rejected** and the team has **corrected** | Module 10 | ☐ |
| A few days of indexing activity, so the KPI charts have data | Module 10 | ☐ |

**Trainer tips**
- Pair a supervisor trainee with a reviewer trainee. The supervisor's sample becomes the reviewer's queue, so both practise on real handovers.
- In Module 7, ask the reviewer to accept every field on one lot (so it passes) and to mark at least one **identifier** field defective on another (a critical defect, so it fails). Module 8 needs the failed lot.
- Module 6 has the trainee reassign a batch. A second reviewer account (or the trainee's own supervisor account) makes this realistic.
- Module 8 needs someone on the digitization side to correct and resubmit the failed lot. Arrange this in advance, or reach that step with a lot you've prepared.

**Module 11.** Observe the trainee on these points:
- They check the inspection report before sampling.
- They read the sample figures before applying.
- They check the line showing how batches will be shared before assigning, and assign every sampled record in the end.
- They wait for DocMate to decide the result, rather than looking for a button.
- They use the tabulation to explain a failure.
- They know switching only uses first rounds.
