# DocMate Customer Portal — Reviewer Training Workbook

**For:** Customer reviewers (QC agents) in training · **Use with:** *Customer Portal — Reviewer User Manual*

This workbook turns the Reviewer User Manual into hands-on practice. Each module has:
- **what you'll learn** — the module's goals;
- **exercises** — practice in the training portal;
- **check your understanding** — questions to answer in the boxes provided;
- a **training record** at the end, where you and your trainer sign off each module.

Work through the modules in order. Allow about **2½ hours** in total.

|---|---|---|---|

> **Training portal only.** Do every exercise in the training portal your trainer gives you, never in your live portal. A **submitted batch can't be taken back**, and its reviews count towards whether a real lot passes.

## Before you start (trainer setup)

*This page is for the trainer. Prepare it before the session.*

| Item | Needed for | Ready |
|---|---|---|
| A **reviewer** (QC agent) account with an unused **temporary password** | Module 1 | ☐ |
| At least **two batches** assigned to the trainee on an **ISO 2859‑1** project, about 8 records in all | Modules 2–4, 6, 9 | ☐ |
| A few records assigned on a **manual-rate** project (pass/reject) | Module 5 | ☐ |
| Records with **known, planted errors** (see the list below), with their record numbers noted on your answer sheet | Modules 4, 7, 9 | ☐ |
| At least one record whose *Encumbrances* (or other list) has an entry, so the trainee sees one line per part of each entry | Module 4 | ☐ |
| A **multi-page** record, if your projects have them | Module 3 | ☐ |
| A **corrected** record that customer QC rejected before, so it shows the yellow banner | Module 3 | ☐ |

**Planted errors.** For the defect-finding exercises, prepare records with one each of:
- a transposed number (for example folio **761** entered as **167**) — a **Typo**;
- a wrong part of a list entry (for example an encumbrance's reference) — a **Typo** on that entry's line only;
- a misspelt name — a **Typo**;
- a value read as something else, or in the wrong field — an **Interpretation** error;
- a field left empty although the card shows a value — **Missing**;
- one record that is **completely correct**.

Put at least one error in an **identifier** field (a field tagged **Identifier**, which makes it a critical defect).

**Trainer tips**
- Pair the trainee with a supervisor trainee working through the *Supervisor Training Workbook*. The supervisor's sample becomes this trainee's queue.
- In the supervisor's Module 7, the supervisor trainee needs one lot reviewed with all fields **accepted** and another with at least one **identifier** field marked defective. Brief this trainee accordingly, and remind them to **submit** each batch: the supervisor can't decide a lot until every batch is submitted.

## Answer key (for trainers)

Remove this section before giving the workbook to trainees, or hand it out after the session.

**Module 1**
- Ex 1.1: The page shows **"Connecting…"**.
- Ex 1.2: Two menu items, **QC Workspace** and **Help**.
- 1.1: Compare each sampled record's indexed data with its image, and say whether it was entered correctly.
- 1.2: Your supervisor, by drawing a **random sample** from each lot.
- 1.3: Your verdicts on the sample decide whether the **whole lot** is accepted or sent back.
- 1.4: After about half an hour without activity you're asked to sign in again. Then open the batch and **Resume** the record.

**Module 2**
- Ex 2.1: The queue refreshes every 30 seconds.
- 2.1: **Open**: nothing reviewed yet. **Continue**: started. **Check & submit**: every record reviewed, ready to check and submit.
- 2.2: **Start QC** begins a record not yet reviewed. **Resume** returns to one you've started but haven't finished. **Review again** reopens one you've already reviewed, to change your verdict before the batch is submitted.
- 2.3: You're up to date. New batches appear automatically.
- 2.4: So your supervisor can find the exact record.

**Module 3**
- Ex 3.1: The blue banner reads *"Record locked by you for QC review."*
- Ex 3.2: The indexed data is read-only. You judge it, you don't correct it.
- Ex 3.3:
  - Blue: the record is yours, so review it.
  - Red: don't review it; go back to the batch and tell your supervisor.
  - Yellow: check carefully that the earlier problem really has been fixed.
- 3.1: When you record your verdict (**Done**, **Pass** or **Reject**). If you leave without recording it, it stays locked to you.
- 3.2: If something is wrong, the record goes back to the team to fix, so there's an audit trail of who changed what.
- 3.3: The field is required.

**Module 4**
- 4.1: The verdict button is **Done**, and there's a **Field Review** card.
- 4.2: It's **rejected**. Once you submit the batch, your supervisor checks the defects and confirms the lot's result; only then does a record that's still rejected go back to the digitization team, with your notes.
- Ex 4.1: The screen shows *"Choose what is wrong"* beside three buttons (Missing, Typo, Interpretation), and **Done** is greyed out until you choose one. After **Done**, the next record in the batch you haven't reviewed opens.
- Ex 4.3: One line per part of the entry, for example **Encumbrances 1 › Type** and **Encumbrances 1 › Reference**. An empty list shows as a single line, **Encumbrances**.
- 4.3: Critical defects usually have zero tolerance: a single one fails the whole lot.
- 4.4: The note is optional, but it helps the team fix the record quickly.
- 4.5: No. You say what was wrong; DocMate works out the class from that, the project's agreement, and whether the field is an identifier.
- 4.6: **Missing**. Change it if something else was wrong.
- 4.7: **Encumbrances 2 › Reference** only. Each part of each entry is a field of its own.

**Module 5**
- 5.1: No. It's required.
- 5.2: Before confirming, click **Cancel**. After confirming, open the record again (**Previous record**, or **Review again** in the batch) and **Pass** it. This works until you submit the batch.
- Ex 5.1: The next record in the batch opens.
- 5.3: No. The current data is kept as a version.

**Module 6**
- Ex 6.1: The record shows **In review**, and the field marks start again from **Accepted**, because marks aren't saved until you click **Done**.
- Ex 6.2: **Previous record** opens the record you reviewed just before, with your marks shown. A new verdict replaces the old one.
- Ex 6.3: *"All records reviewed — check them and press Submit batch when ready."* While a record still needs a review, **Submit batch** is greyed out and says how many records still need one.
- 6.1: No. It's locked to you.
- 6.2: Nobody else can review a record locked to you, and the lot can't be decided until every sampled record is reviewed and every batch submitted. Your supervisor may also move it to someone else, and anything you hadn't recorded is then lost.
- 6.3: No. Only time spent working on the record counts: the record open, the window in front, and some activity in the last few minutes.
- 6.4: Yes, if you haven't submitted that batch: use **Review again**. Once the batch is submitted, tell your supervisor, who can correct the mark when reviewing the lot.
- 6.5: Submitting is final: after it, you can't change any of the batch's reviews.

**Module 7**
- Ex 7.1:
  - a: Defective, **Typo** (transposed number).
  - b: Defective, **Typo** (misspelt name).
  - c: Defective, **Missing** (field empty although the card shows a value).
  - d: Defective, **Missing** for the Date field (it's empty), and **Interpretation** for Remarks if it should be empty (the value was put in the wrong field).
  - e: Accepted.
  - f: **Accepted**. Enter what the card says, even if it looks like a mistake on the original. Check with your supervisor if project rules differ.
  - g: Defective, **Typo**, on **Encumbrances 2 › Reference** only.
  - h: Follow your supervisor's project rule for illegible text. If there isn't one, don't click **Done**; ask, quoting the Record #.
  - i: Defective, **Missing**, on the single **Encumbrances** line (an empty list is one line).
  - j: There's no line for the third entry. Ask your supervisor how to record it, quoting the Record #.
- Ex 7.2: Good notes say what the image shows and what was entered. For example:
  - "Card reads *Hollis*, entered *Hollins*."
  - "Date on card is 12 March 1931, entered 21 March 1931."
  - "Encumbrances entry 3 (mortgage to Westbank, 1934) not entered."
  - "Folio on the card is 761; entered as 167."
  - "Card reads *Westbury*; entered as *Elmstead*."
- 7.1: **Missing**: a value on the image left out (an empty field, an empty list although the card has entries). **Typo**: wrong, extra or swapped characters (a transposed number, a misspelt name). **Interpretation**: the source read or understood wrongly (a misread word, a value in the wrong field).
- 7.2: The image, not your expectations.
- 7.3: Go back to the batch without clicking **Done**, and ask your supervisor, quoting the Record #.
- 7.4: The same standard must apply to every record, so lot results are fair and comparable.

**Module 8**
- Ex 8.1:
  - Unknown Portal: check the address; the first part must be your organisation's portal name.
  - Invalid username or password: retype it, check Caps Lock, and ask your administrator for a reset if needed.
  - Red lock banner: don't review it; go back and tell your supervisor.
  - Start the task to load the image: click **Start QC**.
  - No image available: don't guess; tell your supervisor, quoting the Record #.
  - Blank or slow image: wait, try **Fit Page**, or go back to the batch and **Resume**.
  - Error on **Done**: try again; if it persists, note the message and tell your supervisor.
  - **Done** greyed out: a defective field has no error type yet; choose Missing, Typo or Interpretation for it.
